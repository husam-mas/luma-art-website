# LUMA ART — Designer Brief

**Client:** Luma ART  
**Project Type:** Luxury E-Commerce Platform (B2C + B2B)  
**Timeline:** Design → Development → Launch  
**Language:** English  

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**Luma ART** is a luxury personalized gifts platform specializing in photo engraving and custom design on premium materials. The website must position the brand as a high-end, sophisticated alternative to mass-market personalization services.

**Key Differentiator:** Professional-grade engraving (crystal, wood, metal, acrylic) with a seamless personalization experience for both individual consumers and corporate buyers.

---

## 🏢 Business Overview

### Products & Services

#### 1. **Crystal Collection** (Premium)
- Photo-engraved crystal cubes, hearts, spheres, icebergs
- Custom shapes available
- Crystal keychains
- Price Range: $50–$300+

#### 2. **Wood Collection** (Artisan)
- Hand-engraved designs on natural round wood slices
- Hand-engraved designs on natural square wood slices
- Wooden keychains with names, photos, logos
- Price Range: $30–$150

#### 3. **Metal & Premium Materials**
- Aluminum photo prints and plaques
- Custom aluminum business cards
- Personalized mirrors with engraving
- Price Range: $25–$200

#### 4. **Acrylic & Illuminated Gifts**
- Acrylic photo engravings with wooden LED bases
- Color-changing lighting options
- Price Range: $60–$250

#### 5. **Corporate & Bulk Gifts**
- Branded mugs, cups, water bottles
- Custom notebooks and stationery
- Personalized pens and keychains
- Corporate awards and plaques
- Minimum Order: 50+ units

#### 6. **Event & Wedding Collection**
- Custom acrylic table signs
- Personalized celebration pieces

---

## 👥 Target Audiences

### Primary
1. **Individual Consumers (B2C)**
   - Age: 25–55
   - Income: Mid to high
   - Motivation: Personal memories, gifts for loved ones
   - Behavior: Browse online, upload own photos, expect quick turnaround

2. **Corporate Buyers (B2B)**
   - Decision Makers: Marketing managers, HR directors, procurement teams
   - Budget: $5,000–$50,000+
   - Behavior: Request quotes, require bulk pricing, custom branding

## 🎨 Design Requirements

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Tone:** Luxury, sophisticated, timeless, professional
- **Color Palette:** 
  - Primary: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a)
  - Secondary: Ivory (#f5f5f5)
  - Accent: Champagne gold (#d4af37)
  - Secondary: Forest green (#4a5c3a)

- **Typography:** 
  - Headlines: Elegant serif
  - Body: Modern sans-serif

- **Imagery:** Large, clean product photography with lifestyle context, soft shadows, natural lighting
- **Spacing:** Generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts

### Logo Concept
**"Luma ART"** — "Luma" = light, "ART" = craft & creativity

**Design Direction:**
- Minimalist, modern luxury style
- Geometric diamond/crystal shape with light rays OR abstract brush stroke
- Custom serif letterforms
- Charcoal on ivory primary, gold accents secondary

## 🎨 Design System

### Typography
- **H1:** 56px, serif, charcoal, letter-spacing 2px
- **H2:** 42px, serif, charcoal
- **H3:** 28px, serif, charcoal
- **Body:** 16px, sans-serif, #333
- **Small:** 14px, sans-serif, #666

### Color Palette
| Element | Color | Hex |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Primary Background | Charcoal | #1a1a1a |
| Secondary Background | Ivory | #f5f5f5 |
| Accent | Champagne Gold | #d4af37 |
| Text Primary | Charcoal | #333 |
| Text Secondary | Light Gray | #666 |
| Border | Light Gray | #e0e0e0 |

### Buttons & Components
- **Primary Button:** Gold background, charcoal text
- **Secondary Button:** Transparent, gold border
- **Cards:** Subtle shadow, generous padding (30-40px)
- **Inputs:** Clean border, gold focus state
- **Hover Effects:** Subtle lift + shadow increase
