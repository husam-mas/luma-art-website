A LITTLE GUIDANCE

Thoughtful questions.

Photo & personalization guide →
What can I personalize?

Explore crystal, wood, acrylic, mirrors and aluminum, along with celebration pieces. Business gifts are subject to material and equipment compatibility; the final catalogue requires confirmation.

What makes a good photo or logo?

Choose a clear, well-lit, high-resolution image with an uncluttered subject. PNG or JPEG is supported in this prototype. Production logo formats and artwork requirements will be confirmed before launch.

Will I see the artwork before production?

The intended journey includes artwork approval for photo, logo and custom-layout work. The prototype preview is illustrative, not an engraving proof. Production begins only after the applicable approval step.

Can I order for a business or event?

Yes—use the quote journey to outline quantities, branding, delivery deadlines and packaging. Minimum quantities, pricing and lead times must be confirmed by Luma ART.

Can I place a real order here?

No. This is a local-only design prototype. Nothing is sent or saved to a server, no payment is collected, and no delivery promise is made.
